How pricing is calculated

  1. Baseline: Azure Retail Prices list price (PAYGO) per hour × 730 h/month.
  2. Lifecycle uplift: VMs on a series with a non-zero % pay that % more from the effective date (default 1 Feb 2027) if they are not migrated.
  3. Regional uplift: some regions carry a price increase on new SKUs. It applies to the target SKU's PAYGO price.
  4. No stacking: if a lifecycle uplift applies, it wins; the regional % is not added on top.
  5. Exclusions: Intel v7 families (Dsv7, Ddsv7, Dlsv7, Dldsv7, Esv7, Edsv7) are not subject to the regional uplift.
  6. Reservations: 1- and 3-year RI prices are not uplifted. Windows adds the PAYGO license cost.
  7. Your values: every percentage and date comes from this Settings page. Add a series or region and it is used in the next calculation.

Confirm migration

Downtime: each VM will be deallocated (stopped, compute billing ends, dynamic IPs may change), resized, and restarted. Expect several minutes of downtime per VM.
